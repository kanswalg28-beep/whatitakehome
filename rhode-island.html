Rhode Island paycheck taxes in 2026

State income tax: Progressive (3.75% to 5.99%). Rhode Island uses graduated tax brackets ranging from 3.75% to 5.99%, so higher portions of your wage income are taxed at progressively higher marginal rates.

For a single filer earning $100,000 with no pre-tax deductions, the estimate is $75,706 a year in take-home pay: $13,170 federal income tax, $3,474 Rhode Island income tax and $7,650 Social Security and Medicare, an effective rate of 24.3%.
